# Privacy

Workout Zones requests Health access only after you choose to start a real Watch workout or connect the iPhone app to Health. Preview mode uses bundled example values without Health queries or writes.

The Watch app reads heart rate and saves workouts only after you choose Save. Discarded sessions are not saved. iPhone reads this app’s workout summaries. Apple Health manages storage, permissions, and device synchronization according to your system settings. The app has no server, accounts, advertising, analytics, or external AI service and creates no separate persistent health database.

Health read permissions are intentionally private: empty results do not prove access was denied. You can change permissions or remove saved records through Apple’s Health settings. Uninstalling this app does not delete workouts already saved in Health.
